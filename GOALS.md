# Cloud-Native DevSecOps Lab goals

- Deliver a private-by-default K3s laboratory that provisions independently of existing projects.
- Demonstrate secure delivery and operation of a findings and remediation application, from pull request to signed immutable deployment.
- Validate positive and negative controls using synthetic data and the lab's own workloads.
- Document AWS mappings and publishable evidence with a verified score of at least 90/100 as the acceptance target.

A control earns validation credit when its intended execution path produces an observable result. Configuration review and in-process tests receive credit for their actual scope.
